# Vision Theatre

I'm the logistics and sound guy for a church community theatre. We're doing the story of Ruth this year.

- The scripts are in ./script
- Main characters and their assignments are in ./characters.md
- Available mic equipment is in ./equipment.mds

We have a sound board that I'll be running, an Allen&Heath GLD 112. We're trying to have as many people mic'd up as possible with headsets. We have an aux in, a stage monitor (separate mix from house), and pit mics. I'll have a helper to help me run the mics.
